Launch on Kairo

One transaction creates your coin on Raydium LaunchLab and makes your buy inside it, so the coin is born with your buy. From the next block, anyone can buy. Every launch prints a receipt of its block 0, read from Solana.

  1. One transaction. It creates the coin on Raydium and makes your buy. Your wallet signs it.
  2. Block 0 opens with you. Your buy is inside the create, so nobody can buy before you. Kairo sends no other buy: no bundle, no second wallet.
  3. Block 1 is everyone's. From the next block, anyone can buy on the same curve.
  4. The receipt. Kairo reads block 0 back from Solana and prints who bought in it. Anyone else who landed there is named.
