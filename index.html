03 · How a coin opens

01 / 04 · CREATE

The coin is born with one buy.

The creator launches the coin and makes a single buy in the same transaction. That buy is block 0, from one wallet, out in the open.

02 / 04 · CLOSED

Nothing else lands in block 0.

No bundle, no sniper, no second wallet. Until the opening, every other buy is turned away, whoever pays the biggest tip.

03 / 04 · COUNTDOWN

The opening second is public.

From the moment a coin exists, everyone can see the exact second it opens. Every coin opens on the minute, so the clock is the same for everyone.

04 / 04 · OPEN

At :00, everyone at once.

Trading opens for everyone in the same second. Bots don't get a head start, because there's nothing to start before.

13 · Questions

The questions worth asking.

Is Kairo live?

Partly, and we'd rather say exactly which part. The block 0 scanner, the live openings board, the simulator and the receipt printer work today. Launches open next. Nothing on this site is a live Kairo coin yet.

What is block 0?

The block a coin is created in. On most launchpads that block is open to anyone who can get a transaction in, which is how bundles and snipers take the supply before a person can click.

Can the dev still buy?

Yes, once. The creator's buy rides in the same transaction that creates the coin. It's the only trade in block 0, and it's printed on the coin's receipt for everyone to see.

Why on the minute?

So the opening second is never a secret. Every coin opens at the top of a minute, on a public countdown, and everyone races the same clock.

Can bots still buy at the opening?

They can buy, like anyone. What they lose is the head start: no block 0, no buys before :00. Inside the opening block, order is set by the network.

How does the scanner work?

It reads a coin's history from Solana: the block it was created in, every buy in that block and the next minute, and where each early wallet got its SOL. Nothing is stored and nothing is signed.
