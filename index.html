03 · How a launch works

01 / 04 · CREATE

One transaction, one buy.

You name the coin, add an image and pick your buy. Kairo builds one transaction that creates the coin and makes your buy. You sign it once.

02 / 04 · BLOCK 0

Your buy is alone in block 0.

The coin is born in the same transaction as your buy. Nothing else can land in that block: no bundle, no sniper, no second wallet.

03 / 04 · BOUNCED

Everyone else bounces off block 0.

Bundles and snipers that try to buy in the creation block are refused, however big the tip they pay.

04 / 04 · OPEN

From block 1, anyone can buy.

The very next block is open to everyone on the same curve. No hidden wallets at the bottom, and a receipt that proves it.

13 · Questions

The questions worth asking.

What is Kairo?

A launchpad for Solana coins with one rule that matters most: block 0 is closed. When you launch, your buy is the only one in the block your coin is born in. From the next block, anyone can buy.

What is block 0?

The block a coin is created in. On most launchpads that block is open to anyone who can get a transaction in, which is how bundles and snipers take the supply at the lowest price before a person can click.

Can the dev still buy?

Yes, once in block 0. The creator's buy rides in the same transaction that creates the coin. It's the only trade in block 0, and it's printed on the coin's receipt for everyone to see.

When can I buy?

From block 1, the very next block, about half a second after the coin is born. Everyone buys on the same curve, in the order the network sets.

Can bots still buy?

From block 1, like anyone. What they lose is block 0: no buying in the creation block, no hiding twenty wallets next to the dev at the lowest price.

Is Kairo live?

Partly, and we'd rather say exactly which part. The block 0 scanner, the simulator and the launch form work today. Launches open next. There are no Kairo coins yet.

How do I check a coin?

Paste its contract address into the scanner. It reads the coin's launch straight from Solana: who bought in block 0, who funded them, and how much the dev bought. Nothing is stored and nothing is signed.

Does it cost anything?

The scanner, the simulator and the docs are free. Launch fees will be shown before you sign anything, when launches open.
